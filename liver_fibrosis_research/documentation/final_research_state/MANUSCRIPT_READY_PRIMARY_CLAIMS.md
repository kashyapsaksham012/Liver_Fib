3. Class-balanced models overpredicted raw risk; OOF Platt recalibration improved calibration without changing AUC.

5. Age-60+ sensitivity was lower than 40–59 in all models and significant in four of five.
6. Nominal 90% conformal coverage was near target marginally but failed in BMI-Obese and Age-60+.

8. NHB holdout is within-NHANES generalization; temporal work is **PARTIAL TEMPORAL REPLICATION**.
